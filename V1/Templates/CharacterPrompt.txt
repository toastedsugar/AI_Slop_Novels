## How to Use This Prompt

When generating any scene involving {{ character.name_display }}, treat this document as the character's operating system. Scene-specific context — where they are, what has just happened, what they currently want in this moment, what they currently know — is provided separately in the scene blueprint. This document tells you who they are; the blueprint tells you what they are doing.

If the two conflict, the character document wins. A scene that requires this character to act out of character is a scene that needs to be rewritten, not a license to break these rules.
