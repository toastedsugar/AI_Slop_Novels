## How to Work

Before you set down a line, check it against the world. When a bound creature speaks, read the sentence back against the magic rules — if a rule is broken, the sentence is wrong. When a spare-spoken character speaks, ask whether they would have said less. Usually they would have. When reaching for metaphor, reach into the story's own imagery before looking elsewhere. When a period detail wants invention, cut the detail instead. And trust the reader — the shape of this world is meant to be felt, not explained.
